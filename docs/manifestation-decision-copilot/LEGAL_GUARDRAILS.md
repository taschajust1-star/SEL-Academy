# Legal Guardrails

This system is advisory only and must not replace team-based decisions required under IDEA, Section 504, ADA, FERPA, or Minnesota law.

## Required Disclaimer

This tool provides structured decision support. It does not replace required team-based determination under IDEA, Section 504, or district policy.

## Prohibited Uses

- Determining manifestation
- Assigning discipline
- Diagnosing or labeling

## Required Prompts

- Was the conduct caused by, or did it have a direct and substantial relationship to the student’s disability?
- Was the conduct the direct result of failure to implement the IEP?

## Data Privacy

- Follow FERPA
- Limit identifiable data
- Maintain audit logs

## Ethical Standard

Support over punishment. Context over labeling.
