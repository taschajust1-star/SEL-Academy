## 1. Executive Summary {#executive-summary}

Just 1 Team is an educational ecosystem company operating under two deeply integrated pillars — SEL Academy and Mashkawiziiwin. Our core promise to Special Education Directors is simple and urgent: **We reduce director overload and stabilize staff in 30 days.**

The global Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) market is projected to grow from $2.71 billion in 2026 to over $27 billion by 2033. Yet despite this explosive growth, Special Education Directors across the country report that their teachers are burning out faster than they can be replaced, their compliance obligations are paralyzing, and their students — particularly those from neurodiverse and Indigenous backgrounds — are being failed by generic, culturally tone-deaf interventions.

Just 1 Team does not compete in the crowded SEL vendor market. We are a **pre-language market creator**, defining a new category entirely: **Culturally-Flexible Ecosystem Regulation**. We deliver this through:
- A proprietary neurochemical framework (DOES-C)
- An Indigenous wisdom tradition (Mashkawiziiwin)
- Highly specific, demo-able AI-augmented strategic coaching

## 2. The Problem: A System in Crisis {#the-problem}

Special education in the United States is in a state of compounding crisis. More than 7 million students — approximately 14% of all public school students — are served under IDEA.

| Crisis Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **Staffing Crisis** | Teacher shortages persist nationwide. Burnout is a structural outcome, not a personal failing. |
| **Compliance Burden** | Directors spend the majority of their time on IEP documentation and legal compliance, leaving no bandwidth for strategic leadership. |
| **Cultural Gap** | Indigenous students, students of color, and neurodiverse learners are disproportionately misidentified, over-disciplined, and underserved. |
| **Root Cause** | No one is regulating the nervous systems of the adults in the building. |

**First Principles Truth:** A dysregulated nervous system cannot teach, lead, or learn.

## 7. Case Study: The Proof Layer {#case-study}

**Flagship Case Narrative: Alternative Education District Transformation**
*(Based on foundational work with districts like ISD 287 and Brooklyn Center)*

- **Before:** High-needs alternative district experiencing severe staff burnout, high turnover, and escalating daily behavioral incidents. Administration was entirely reactive, spending 80% of time managing adult dysregulation.
- **Intervention:** Implemented DOES-C framework and ROYGBIV color-coded regulation system across staff. Provided the Special Education Director with targeted strategic coaching to shift from compliance-manager to ecosystem-regulator.
- **After:** Within 60 days, staff reported significant decrease in daily stress. Behavioral escalations de-escalated faster using shared neurochemical language (DOES-C).

> *"For the first time, we aren't just putting out fires. We have a shared language for our own nervous systems, which has completely changed how we respond to our students."* — District Administrator
