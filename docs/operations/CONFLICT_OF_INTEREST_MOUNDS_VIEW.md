# Conflict of Interest & "Do Not Sell" Policy: Mounds View ISD 621

**Entity:** Just 1 Team LLC (DBA SEL Academy | ManifestIQ)  
**Effective Date:** May 5, 2026  
**Owner:** Tascha Just, Founder & CEO  
**Statutory Basis:** Minnesota Statute § 471.87 (Public Officers, Interest in Contract)

---

## 1. Core Policy Statement

Tascha Just, Founder and CEO of Just 1 Team LLC (DBA SEL Academy and ManifestIQ), currently serves as an elected school board member for **Mounds View Public Schools (ISD 621)**. 

Under Minnesota state law (Minn. Stat. § 471.87), a public officer is strictly prohibited from having a personal financial interest in any sale, lease, or contract with the public body they serve. 

To ensure absolute compliance, protect the integrity of Mounds View ISD 621, and safeguard the operational viability of Just 1 Team LLC, the following strict operational boundaries are enacted immediately.

---

## 2. The "Do Not Sell To" List

**Mounds View Public Schools (ISD 621) is permanently placed on the "Do Not Sell To" list for all products, services, and software offered by Just 1 Team LLC, SEL Academy, and ManifestIQ.**

This prohibition applies to all current and future offerings, including but not limited to:
* ManifestIQ SaaS subscriptions or pilots
* SEL Academy Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 consulting packages
* District Discipline Risk Audits
* Paid speaking engagements, keynotes, or workshops
* Prism Practitioner Certification licensing
* Any customized curriculum or training packages

This prohibition remains in effect for the entire duration of Tascha Just's tenure on the Mounds View ISD 621 school board.

## 3. Operator & Sales Representative Training Notice

All incoming operators, integrators, business development (BD) representatives, and commission-based sales staff must read and acknowledge this policy prior to commencing work or outreach.

**Mandatory Rules of Engagement for Sales & BD:**
1. **Zero Outreach:** No sales emails, calls, or marketing materials may be directed to any administrator, director, principal, or staff member employed by Mounds View ISD 621.
2. **Inbound Inquiry Deflection:** If an inquiry is received from a Mounds View ISD 621 employee, it must be immediately flagged to the Integrator/COO and politely declined citing this conflict of interest policy.
3. **Territory Exclusion:** Mounds View ISD 621 is explicitly excluded from all sales territories and commission calculations.
4. **Immediate Termination of Process:** Any operator or sales representative found actively attempting to circumvent this policy or sell to Mounds View ISD 621 will be immediately disqualified from any Revenue-Share Tournaments or Operator-in-Residence trials, and their contract will be terminated.
