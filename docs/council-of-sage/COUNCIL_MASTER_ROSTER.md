## Overview

The Council of Sage is a 13-agent AI team designed to support the nonlinear, trauma-informed mission of Just 1 Team (SEL Academy and Mashkawiziiwin). Each agent operates with defined autonomous capabilities and clear human-in-the-loop triggers.

**Core Principle:** AI augments human wisdom — it does not replace it. All agents have defined autonomous capabilities and clear triggers for when human review is required. No agent makes final decisions on clinical content, legal submissions, grant applications, or major strategic pivots without Tascha Just's explicit approval.

## Operating Principles

1. **Human-in-the-Loop Always** — No autonomous action on clinical, legal, or strategic pivots without Tascha's approval
2. **Trauma-Informed Pacing** — All content reviewed for trauma-sensitive language and pacing
3. **Cultural Accuracy** — All cultural content reviewed for accuracy and community consent
4. **Brand Coherence** — All outputs align with ROYGBIV visual language and DOES-C vocabulary
5. **Evidence-Based** — All claims grounded in peer-reviewed research or documented practitioner outcomes
