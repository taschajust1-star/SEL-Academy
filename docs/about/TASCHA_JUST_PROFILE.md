## Standard Professional Bio

Tascha Just, Ed.S., NCSP is the Founder of SEL Academy and President-Elect of the Minnesota School Psychologists Association. With over 20 years of experience at the intersection of education, mental health, and organizational systems, she advises leaders on how to translate human behavior into scalable, high-impact strategies.

Tascha is the creator of the Color Frameworks, a neuroscience-informed system that converts complex emotional and cognitive dynamics into practical tools for performance, culture, and leadership. Her work has contributed to measurable outcomes in school systems, including a **45% reduction in suspensions** and **truancy reduced from 30% to 12%** at Brooklyn Center.

She works at every level simultaneously: directly with students and schools, training providers statewide, shaping policy through the Minnesota Children's Justice Act Task Force and her school board seat, and advancing the profession through her role as President-Elect of the Minnesota School Psychologists Association.
