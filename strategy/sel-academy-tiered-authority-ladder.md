# SEL Academy Tiered Authority Ladder

## Strategic Positioning

**Primary niche:** Neurodivergent-Compatible Human Systems for K-12 leadership and student support teams.

**Core promise:** SEL Academy helps schools and leadership teams build neurodivergent-compatible systems that reduce burnout, improve regulation, and strengthen implementation in high-stress environments.

**Big idea:** Designed for human nervous systems, not just organizational charts.

## Tier 0: Free — Signal & Recognition

### Purpose
Help leaders and staff recognize the problem before they are ready to buy.

### Sample assets
- Short videos on regulation, burnout, implementation fatigue, neurodivergent-compatible systems, and leadership overload
- LinkedIn posts and carousels
- Free checklist: **Signs Your System Is Dysregulated**
- Free guide: **Neurodivergent-Compatible Leadership Checklist**
- Free PDF: **10 SELa Repair Scripts for Hard Moments With Young People**

### Conversion goal
Move people from: "Who is SEL Academy?" to "This explains what we are experiencing."
